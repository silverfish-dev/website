Open Source for Regulated Industries

Built around the principle "Safe. Open. Continuously.", Silverfish Development helps organizations adopt Open Source in embedded products with confidence. Services are designed for regulated environments and combine technical enablement, governance, and practical rollout support across engineering, product, and compliance teams.

Embedded Linux Products

Embedded Linux services cover the full product lifecycle, from architecture and build pipelines to release management, update strategies, and long-term maintenance. The result is a delivery model that keeps products safe, open, and continuously improved in real-world operation.

Safety-Critical Open Source

Safety-critical Open Source is a core focus, with services tailored to regulated and high-assurance domains. Practical, traceable, and audit-ready processes support certification goals while enabling continuous engineering progress.

Open Source Strategy

Open Source strategy services connect engineering, product management, and leadership through clear objectives and realistic execution models. Companies can get practical advice to align teams, reduce risk, and turn Open Source into a reliable business capability.
